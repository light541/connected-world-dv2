CHAPTER 03 / BRAND CHOICE

Not every market chooses alike.

Device vendors differ across regions. Statcounter measures mobile web-browsing share attributed to each vendor, offering a useful — but imperfect — view of phones in active use. These percentages are not units sold, survey preferences, or customer ratings.

CHAPTER 04 / THE AFTERLIFE

The other side of consumption.

Phones, computers and other electronics eventually reach the end of their lives. The formal recycling share measures the fraction of recorded electronic waste entering documented recycling systems — a narrower idea than all reuse, recovery, or informal handling.

Final perspective

Connection is not the full story.

Connectivity, device access and formal recycling cannot be collapsed into one ranking. These indicators measure different things, in different years. Viewed together, they invite a more responsible question: how do we expand access to technology while designing for longer device lifetimes and better recovery?

DATA & METHODOLOGY

Sources, definitions and limitations

The charts fetch published CSV datasets directly from Our World in Data, with attribution to the original collecting organisations. This implementation does not fabricate missing observations. Check source pages for revisions after the publication date.

  • Individuals using the internet — ITU World Telecommunication/ICT Indicators Database, via World Bank and OWID. Percent of population.
  • Mobile phone subscriptions per 100 people — ITU via World Bank and OWID. Subscriptions, not unique smartphone owners or devices sold.
  • Electronic waste recycling rate — UN Statistics Division and UNEP via OWID. Formal collection/recycling of all e-waste, not specifically phones.
  • Population by country — UN World Population Prospects via OWID, used only in the treemap’s transparent population × share estimate.
  • Mobile vendor browsing share (September 2026) — Statcounter Global Stats, with individual source pages for Malaysia, Asia, Europe, India and the United States. The bundled CSV transcribes each page’s six displayed vendors; unlisted brands are not imputed.
  • World map geometry — Vega Datasets; country coordinates are approximate geographic references from the CountryInfo package.

Reading the charts: 2024 indicators are not claimed to describe smartphone sales or ownership; some countries lack data. Region tiles are schematic, and the year of the 2022 recycling observations differs from the year of the 2024 connectivity observations. Circle areas denote subscription rates. The treemap contains only the largest paired 2023 estimates. CSVs are delivered by their publishers at page-load time and require an internet connection.
